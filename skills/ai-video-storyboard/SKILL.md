# AI Video Storyboard, Screenplay & Hyper-Precision Prompting Master Engine (v4.0)

## Overview

The `ai-video-storyboard` skill is a complete, studio-grade Hollywood production workflow designed specifically for AI filmmaking and short-form content. It eliminates the fatal flaws of generative video—such as character morphing, shoulder/wound flipping, impossible spatial reaches, phantom prop duplication, and fabric tearing—by enforcing **1:1 Visual-to-Kinematic Micro-Detail Binding and Spatial Geometry Auditing** between generated storyboard images and video prompts.

---

## The Ten Core Production Pillars

### 1. 1:1 Visual-to-Kinematic Binding & Spatial Geometry Audit
Video diffusion models cannot execute inverse kinematics or impossible 3D locomotion. Before writing any video prompt, the engine performs a mandatory **5-Point Spatial Geometry Audit** of the anchor image (`bridge_frame.jpg`):
1. **Pose & Posture Geometry**: Exact physical posture of human and animal (e.g., kneeling on both knees, lying prone on belly with front paws outstretched).
2. **Hand Coordinates & Prop Anchor**: Exact 2D/3D location of each hand and what it holds (e.g., right hand at chest height holding gauze, left hand resting on knee).
3. **Spatial Reachability Gate**: Calculates distance between acting limb and target:
   - *Reach $\le$ Arm Length (~15-20% frame width)*: Direct planar motion permitted.
   - *Reach > Arm Length*: The prompt MUST NOT command an immediate touch across the void! The director MUST cut to a tighter **Medium Action Shot / OTS coverage** where the limb is already adjacent, OR choreograph full-body displacement before contact.
4. **Target Anatomical Localization**: Explicitly names the exact body part (e.g., "circular reddish patch on camera-facing shoulder blade on Screen-Right").
5. **Spatial Negative Exclusion**: Barring neighboring anatomy to prevent misdirected inpainting (e.g., "STRICT NEGATIVE: Do NOT place bandage on neck or ear; neck remains 100% clean white fur").

---

### 2. Planar Interaction Physics (Anti-Toroidal & Anti-Flipping Protocol)
Prevents subjects or wounds from flipping to the wrong side:
- **Ban on 3D Rotational Wrapping Verbs**: Forbids words like `wraps around the joint, encircles the leg, wraps behind`. These force diffusion models to reveal the occluded 3D backside, spinning the subject or jumping the 180° camera line.
- **Planar Surface Interactions**: Replaces wrapping with planar actions: `places flat directly over the wound`, `presses the sterile pad flat against the fur`, `smooths adhesive strips across the surface`.
- **Absolute 180° Camera Axis Lock**: Permanently locks Screen-Left and Screen-Right character assignments with zero reverse-angle cuts.

---

### 3. Prop Transfer & Anti-Duplication Mechanics
Prevents diffusion models from duplicating objects (holding one copy in hand while another appears on the target):
- Explicitly details the hand release state:
  `Maya releases the white gauze pad onto the wound, smoothing it flat with empty black-gloved fingers. Zero duplicate gauze remains in her hands; her empty gloves rest gently on the secure dressing.`

---

### 4. 4-Step Anatomical Mechanical Causality (Purging Negative Lexicon Traps)
Eliminates objects popping out of solid clothing (e.g., bags materializing from chest fabric):
- **4-Step Mechanical Chain**:
  1. Hand moves to specific zipper pull-tab on seam.
  2. Hand pulls zipper downward along the seam line, parting the fabric fold.
  3. Hand slides inside the opened pocket cavity.
  4. Hand withdraws object outward through the opened slit into open air.
- **Purge Negative Lexicon Traps**: Never write `(she does not pull it from the middle)` in the positive prompt! Text encoders latch onto the negated words and manifest them. Positive, sequential causality only.

---

### 5. Aspect-Ratio-Aware Storyboard Grids
Generates storyboard sheets tailored specifically to the target video delivery format:
- **16:9 Widescreen Mode (YouTube, Cinema, Television)**:
  - Formatted in an official **4-Column × 3-Row Grid (12 panels total)** on a 16:9 canvas.
  - **Panels ① to ⑩**: Sequential widescreen 16:9 live-action movie panels.
  - **Panels ⑪ & ⑫**: Integrated technical production slate cards:
    - *Panel ⑪*: `AUDIO DNA PROFILE` (Dialogue timing, mix ratios, acoustic harmonics).
    - *Panel ⑫*: `DIRECTOR LOGIC AUDIT` (Verification stamp `[PASSED]`).
- **9:16 Vertical Mode (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts)**:
  - Generates **Two Vertical Storyboard Sheets (5+5 Layout)** to prevent squishing vertical panels into unreadable thumbnails:
    - *Sheet 1*: Panels ① to ⑤ (Seconds 0:00 to 0:05) in full 9:16 vertical resolution.
    - *Sheet 2*: Panels ⑥ to ⑩ (Seconds 0:05 to 0:10) in full 9:16 vertical resolution.

### 6. The Master Screenplay & Scriptwriting Engine
Full-spectrum cinematic writing engine for any video format:
- **Cinematic Films & Series**: Scene headings (`INT./EXT.`), character beats, subtextual dialogue, atmospheric action blocks.
- **Viral Reels & TikTok**: 3-second visual/vocal hook, retention bridge, emotional climax, seamless loop anchor, and high-converting CTA.
- **Commercials & Video Ads (AIDA)**: Attention (0-3s), Interest (3-8s), Desire (8-20s), Action (20-30s).
- **Auto-Segmentation**: Automatically breaks any completed script into 10-second production segments.

---

### 7. The Unified Single-File Contract (`prompts.txt`)
**Zero file clutter**. Every segment folder contains exactly ONE comprehensive text file named `prompts.txt` containing six clearly labeled sections:
1. `[NARRATIVE REALITY & CHARACTER DNA LOCK]`
2. `[SPATIAL GEOMETRY & ANCHOR AUDIT]` (Spatial coordinates, reachability gate, exclusions)
3. `PART 1: MASTER STORYBOARD SHEET PROMPT` (4×3 or 5+5 sheet prompt)
4. `PART 2: INSTANT VIDEO ENGINE PROMPT` (100% safety-compliant, ready-to-paste prompt)
5. `PART 3: SEQUENTIAL SECOND-BY-SECOND BREAKDOWN` (Frames 01 to 10)
6. `PART 4: DIRECTOR'S LOGICAL & PHYSICAL SANITY AUDIT` (5-point verification report)

### 8. Automated Safety-Shield Engine (Gemini & Runway Policy Compliance)
Bypasses false-positive policy rejections (*"I can't make videos that could relate to dangerous situations"*) by converting dramatic tension into compliant Hollywood stunt language:
- **Set Framing**: *"Scripted cinematic movie scene from a fictional family-friendly feature film on an authorized studio set"*.
- **Theatrical Stunt Makeup**: *"distinct circular reddish theatrical stunt makeup marks"* or *"simulated prosthetic scratches"*.
- **Cooperative Companions**: *"docile, cooperative, trained animal companions in a serene wildlife rescue scene"*.
- **Automated Keyword Ban**: Replaces dangerous terms (`blood, gore, attack, violent, lethal, fatal, slaughter`) with compliant visual descriptions.

### 9. Anti-Morphing Single Continuous Take Protocol
- Enforces a single, unbroken camera motion across the 10 seconds.
- Forbids rapid cuts to tight facial close-ups inside a short clip.
- Strict Reference Image Lock: `STRICT CONTINUITY & CHARACTER IDENTITY LOCK: Unbroken single-take continuation starting directly from the uploaded reference image. The character MUST PRESERVE HER EXACT FACIAL IDENTITY, BONE STRUCTURE, AND APPEARANCE FROM THE REFERENCE IMAGE. Zero character morphing.`

## Step-by-Step Production Protocol

```
1. SCRIPT / CONCEPT     --> Write or adapt screenplay using the Scriptwriting Engine.
2. SEGMENTATION         --> Break script into sequential 10-second segments.
3. BRIDGE FRAME ANCHOR  --> Secure bridge_frame.jpg (from previous video or sheet).
4. SPATIAL AUDIT        --> Run 5-point spatial audit (reachability, anchor points, exclusions).
5. UNIFIED PROMPTS      --> Compose prompts.txt with planar physics & anti-duplication.
6. STORYBOARD SHEET     --> Generate 4x3 sheet (16:9) or dual 5+5 sheets (9:16).
7. VIDEO GENERATION     --> Copy-paste Part 2 prompt into Gemini / Kling / Runway.
8. MARKETING POSTERS    --> Generate 16:9 & 9:16 posters + bilingual social copy.
```
